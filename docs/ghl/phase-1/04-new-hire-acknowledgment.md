# NEW HIRE ONBOARDING ACKNOWLEDGMENT

I, **[AGENT LEGAL NAME]** (**“Sales Partner”**), acknowledge the following in connection with my onboarding with **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, doing business as **Mercury Call Desk** (**“Company”**).

## 1. Onboarding Completion

I understand that I may not represent Company, contact Company leads or customers, access production systems, or become eligible for payouts until Company confirms that my onboarding is complete. Company may require completion of all assigned documents, account verification, approved training, system-access setup, tax documentation, and any additional compliance step before activation.

## 2. Governing Documents

I acknowledge that I have received or will receive the following documents and that I am responsible for reading and following them:

- Sales Partner Agreement;
- NDA / Confidentiality and IP Agreement;
- W-9 / Payout Intake instructions;
- current Company commission plan or individual rate card;
- Company sales, communication, privacy, data-security, and customer-service policies; and
- any approved scripts, product materials, compliance notices, and process updates issued by Company.

If there is a conflict between an informal communication and a signed agreement, written commission plan, or formally published Company policy, the signed agreement, current written plan, or formal policy controls.

## 3. System Access and Information Security

I will keep my login credentials private, use only my authorized user account, enable multi-factor authentication when available, and never share access with another person. I will not download, export, copy, store, transmit, screen-record, photograph, or use Company or customer data except as expressly authorized through Company systems.

I will promptly report a lost device, suspected account compromise, suspicious login, phishing attempt, misdirected message, unauthorized access, or other security concern to **[SECURITY / NOTICE EMAIL]**.

## 4. Sales and Customer Conduct

I understand that I may use only Company-approved systems, scripts, pricing, branding, materials, and claims. I will not:

- guarantee an outcome, approval, savings amount, implementation date, pricing exception, or service result;
- make a statement that is false, misleading, unsubstantiated, or inconsistent with Company-approved materials;
- collect payment information or sensitive personal information outside approved Company systems;
- contact a person who has opted out or otherwise instructed Company not to contact them;
- make unauthorized changes to a customer account, invoice, package, price, or agreement; or
- present myself as a Company employee, owner, lender, attorney, tax adviser, or person authorized to bind Company.

I will document material customer communications, requests, issues, renewals, escalations, and resolutions in Company systems as required.

## 5. Account Servicing and Commissions

I understand that an account that is current on payment and has no open issue is not reassigned solely because there was no routine quarterly check-in. My servicing performance is evaluated through account activity and timely, documented responses to customer requests, support needs, payment issues, renewal events, escalations, and resolutions.

I understand that commissions are governed by the Sales Partner Agreement and the applicable written commission plan or rate card. No verbal statement creates a commission, payout right, guaranteed earnings level, customer ownership right, or exception to a written policy.

## 6. Changes, Audits, and Cooperation

Company may revise operational policies, scripts, systems, or training requirements prospectively when reasonably necessary for compliance, customer experience, product changes, or business operations. I agree to complete reasonable refresher training and cooperate with reasonable compliance reviews or investigations.

## 7. Acknowledgment

By signing below, I confirm that I have read and understand this acknowledgment. I agree to follow Company’s written onboarding, operational, security, and compliance requirements. I understand that violations may result in suspension of access, reassignment of accounts, loss of eligibility for future unearned commissions where permitted by applicable agreements and law, or termination of my relationship with Company.
